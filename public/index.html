METHODOLOGY & SOURCES

What the public mock board shows—and what it does not

This site intentionally uses a narrow public dataset: ESPN average draft position and raw 2026 statistical projections. It does not expose private rankings, watchlists, injury models, tiers, or draft recommendations.

ADP

ESPN only, without substitutions

ADP comes directly from ESPN Fantasy Football’s averageDraftPosition value. The table does not blend ESPN with consensus ADP, expert rank, or this league’s keeper-compressed board. An em dash means ESPN did not publish a usable value for that player.

2026 PROJECTED STATISTICS

Raw opportunity and production—not a private fantasy score

Season totals are divided by the provider’s projected games to create the compact per-game lines shown in the player table. Passing, rushing, receiving, kicking, and team-defense categories are displayed as raw statistics. The site does not convert them into NYFL fantasy points or a proprietary player grade.
